Use case: illustration-story

Create one vertical 9:16 cinematic keyframe inside a handcrafted Wayuu paper-cut world. Every visible element is made from thin matte dyed cut-paper sheets: broad silhouettes, exposed internal sheet edges, overlapping layers, air gaps and cast shadows. The ground is made from large flat irregular paper pieces, never realistic sand, soil, clay or stone. Humans are almost-flat paper silhouettes with natural adult proportions, complete modest clothing, minimal profile faces and no sculpted skin. Full bleed inside the paper world; no craft box, tabletop or exterior border.

Scene: an adult woman walks slowly and upright across a broad, completely flat patio in front of a simple paper rancheria house. Two adult neighbors are very distant on a separate depth plane and turn their heads toward her without approaching. Their restrained posture and large physical separation are the only social signal. All three figures must be fully visible and small, each occupying only 12 to 18 percent of total image height. Side-on long shot from human eye level, strong horizontal spacing, open sky, no foreground human.

Palette: muted terracotta, charcoal gray, off-white and deep blue sky. No vegetation, flowerpots, sea, lagoon, text, symbols, ropes, weapons, dangerous edges, suspended objects, body-shaped shadows or extra props. No aggression, pursuit or physical contact. One stable ordinary moment only. No collage, labels, captions, watermark or frame.

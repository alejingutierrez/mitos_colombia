Use case: illustration-story

CONTRATO MATERIAL INVARIABLE — PAPERCUT: foreground, characters, props, vegetation, ground, sky and magical phenomenon are ALL clearly constructed from thin matte dyed CUT-PAPER SHEETS. Broad deliberately cut silhouettes and exposed INTERNAL sheet edges, subtle paper grain, visible overlaps, AIR GAPS and cast shadows between foreground, middle and distant layers. Ground is large overlapping flat irregular paper cutouts, never granular sand, realistic soil, clay, cracked mud or a stone surface with paper texture pasted on. Rocks are folded/stacked paper strata. Plants have flat cut stems and leaves, not realistic woody branches. Pots and bowls, if requested, are curved assembled PAPER pieces with thin visibly overlapping rims, not smooth rendered ceramic. Water, rain, clouds, fire and supernatural effects are cut-paper shapes on distinct depth planes, not photographic fluid, smoke or CGI particles. Humans are almost FLAT one-sheet adult/child-appropriate silhouettes, flat side-profile faces with contour-only nose and minimal cut eye, no sculpted or faceted heads, no volumetric skin, no Pixar. Clothing is a few broad paper pieces with full contextual outfits; no printed texture used to imitate paper. Maintain cinematic depth and visually rich relationships without miniature-photography realism. Full bleed INSIDE this paper world: no exterior cardboard base, craft box, tabletop, frame or corrugation. This material contract remains unchanged in every shot and every correction; only action, composition, camera and local color/light change.

Asset type: ficha de identidad visual adicional de la Biblia de El guerrero Ipuana; una sola vista, no collage ni hoja con múltiples figuras.

Subject: Botella de ron ordinaria de contorno ámbar oscuro, cuello estrecho y base sencilla, construida con dos hojas planas mate y borde interno fino; cero vidrio realista, marca o etiqueta legible. Sin boca, ojos, cara o consumo. La voz se traduce en una sola cinta de papel que sale del cuello, licencia editorial.

Pose: una única botella de contorno plano ámbar sobre el suelo; de su cuello sale una cinta estrecha de papel ocre suave, apenas una curva y sin texto. Sin rostro, boca, ojos, líquido visible ni reflejos de vidrio.

Scene: ambiente neutro hecho de tres planos de papel: grandes hojas crudas superpuestas en suelo, una hoja gris cálido al medio y fondo azul gris claro. Aire y sombras entre capas, ninguna planta o casa. Vista frontal lateral con sujeto al centro, margen suficiente; sin marco, base exterior, mesa o caja.

Lighting: lateral suave para revelar espesor de hojas, acabado mate; nunca fotografía de miniatura o juguete esculpido.

Constraints: identidad particular para este mito. Color es decisión editorial, no dato histórico. No reutilizar poderes ni símbolos de otros relatos. Sin texto, etiquetas, logotipos, números o marcas de agua.

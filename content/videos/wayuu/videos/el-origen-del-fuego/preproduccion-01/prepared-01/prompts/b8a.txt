CONTRATO MATERIAL INVARIABLE — PAPERCUT: foreground, characters, props, vegetation, ground, sky and magical phenomenon are ALL clearly constructed from thin matte dyed CUT-PAPER SHEETS. Broad deliberately cut silhouettes and exposed INTERNAL sheet edges, subtle paper grain, visible overlaps, AIR GAPS and cast shadows between foreground, middle and distant layers. Ground is large overlapping flat irregular paper cutouts, never granular sand, realistic soil, clay, cracked mud or a stone surface with paper texture pasted on. Pots and bowls, if requested, are curved assembled PAPER pieces with thin visibly overlapping rims, not smooth rendered ceramic. Water, rain, clouds, fire and supernatural effects are cut-paper shapes on distinct depth planes, not photographic fluid, smoke or CGI particles. Humans are almost FLAT one-sheet adult/child-appropriate silhouettes, flat side-profile faces with contour-only nose and minimal cut eye, no sculpted or faceted heads, no volumetric skin, no Pixar. Clothing is a few broad paper pieces with full contextual outfits; no printed texture used to imitate paper. Maintain cinematic depth and visually rich relationships without miniature-photography realism. Full bleed INSIDE this paper world: no exterior cardboard base, craft box, tabletop, frame or corrugation. This material contract remains unchanged in every shot and every correction; only action, composition, camera and local color/light change.
